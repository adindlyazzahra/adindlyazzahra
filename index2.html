Welcome to Adinda’s Works Binder!

Adinda (@adindlyazzahra - Instagram, Gmail) created this vibecoded website on November 27, 2026, to archive her works, including pieces created before and after its launch.

To explore the archive, you’ll need to import a JSON file containing Adinda’s artwork. You can download the file here. Make sure to download the latest version, as it contains the most up-to-date artwork and revisions. And also beware of malicious files. I am trying my best to keep the site and the archive safe. After downloading, you can import the file using the "Import backup" button on the top-right corner of this site.

Adinda tries her best to keep AI out of her creative process. However, when she does use it for purposes such as improving efficiency or checking anatomical accuracy, she’ll be transparent about it. You can find information about AI involvement in each work page’s description.

Sorry for the extra steps involved in downloading a file to get the full experience. I personally think this is the most practical workflow for maintaining the archive. It allows me to update the work simply by editing the JSON file, without having to push website updates every time I add or revise something. This is especially convenient since I don’t get to use my laptop very often. And I am sorry for my usage of AI in some of my work. I will keep trying to improve so that I can make my own original works.

Thank you for visiting and taking the time to explore my work!
